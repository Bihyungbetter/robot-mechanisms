01 / 05

Drivetrain

Tank

  • Built for a defensive robot
  • Full traction wheels resist being pushed
  • Strong pushing power
  • Simple and durable
  • Cannot strafe; must turn to reposition

Mecanum

  • Built for mobility
  • Strafes and moves in any direction
  • Fast alignment to scoring and intake spots
  • Less traction; easier to push around
  • Rollers wear and need upkeep

Mecanum Setup

  • Wheels should form an X shape
  • Belt drive to relieve stress on motors
  • Commonly seen with the parallel drivetrain

Built forSpeed

  • Fast drivetrain is a top priority
  • Gear for top speed: 1100 RPM motors at a 2:1 ratio
  • Keep the robot light to hold acceleration
  • Trade-off: faster gearing means less pushing force
02 / 05

Intake

IntakeGoals

  • Very fast and reliable
  • Picks up from corners
  • Adjusts between nectar and pollen
  • Intake directly from flowers

Intake

What to use
  • Compliant wheels
  • Surgical tubing
  • Rubber bands
  • Zip ties
  • Funnel / mecanum wheels
Loose bar intake
  • The bar can raise up to let balls in more easily
  • Could allow for nectar and pollen
Two intakes
  • A separate intake on the front and back of the robot
  • Double the mechanisms
  • Takes up a lot of space
  • Reliable, but could increase cycle times

Intake for Flowers

  • Ramp in front of the robot lets pollen roll into the robot
  • Great for autonomous, since pollen starts in flowers
  • Easy to implement on any robot
03 / 05

Shooting

Catapult

  • Shoots all balls in an instant
  • Great for adapting to different ball sizes
  • Bad for shooting from different locations

Flywheel

  • Consistent; the main design for DECODE
  • Can have a turret to shoot from anywhere
  • Could have slower shooting time
  • Could be more reliable

Turret

  • Precise aiming
  • Aim without turning the drivetrain
  • Can shoot while driving
  • More complex, heavier, needs tracking code

Stationary

  • Simplicity
  • Fewer parts, lighter, more reliable
  • Easier to build and tune
  • Whole robot must turn to aim
04 / 05

Transfer

Sorting Options

Spindexer
  • Rotating indexer sorts the balls
  • One intake feeds it
  • Compact storage
  • Needs sensing and sort logic
Two paths
  • One intake splits into two paths
  • Balls routed by type
  • Mostly passive once sorted
  • Needs a gate or diverter
Two intakes
  • A separate intake and transfer for each ball
  • No sorting needed
  • Double the mechanisms
  • Most weight and space
05 / 05

FlowerMechanism

Flower Options

Extending hood
  • Hood extends to reach the flower
  • Builds on the shooter
  • Few extra parts
Capping claw
  • Claw caps the flower with nectar
  • Precise placement
  • Needs its own actuator
Dumper
  • Mechanism lifts up and dumps
  • Simple motion
  • Needs lift height and time
